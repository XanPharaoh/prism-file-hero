Questions, answered.

Do my documents get uploaded to a server?+

Standard PDF tools run entirely in your browser. Financial and AI tools only route a file through processing when a document genuinely needs it, and the desktop and offline suites keep everything on your machine.

Is Prism only a bank-statement tool?+

Far from it. Statements are one lane. The everyday product is 37 document tools: merge, split, compress, repair, redact, OCR, convert, compare, summarise and more. The financial engine is what you graduate to when the work is serious.

Which banks and fund managers are supported?+

Fourteen profiles including FNB, Capitec, Nedbank, ABSA, Standard Bank, TymeBank, Discovery, Investec, Bidvest, African Bank and ICICI, plus Old Mutual, Sanlam and Allan Gray portfolio statements.

Can I trust the numbers enough to file with them?+

Ledger Guard applies five deterministic checks: balance recompute, debit/credit isolation, outlier detection, duplicate prevention and a closing proof. Each row carries a confidence flag, so anything uncertain surfaces for you rather than hiding in the total.

Is the categorisation tied to South African tax?+

Yes. Rules map onto SARS VAT 501 boxes, handle 15% output and input VAT, the registration threshold, PAYE/UIF and the small-asset write-off, in English and Afrikaans. Unmatched lines go to human review, never a silent guess.
