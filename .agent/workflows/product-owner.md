# /product-owner - Product Ownership & Strategy

$ARGUMENTS

---

## Purpose

Invoke the Product Owner for:
- Backlog management and prioritization
- MVP definition and planning
- Strategic product decisions
- Release planning
- Stakeholder management
- Product vision refinement

---

## Usage

```
/product-owner help me define the MVP for [product]
/product-owner manage backlog for [project]
/product-owner plan release for [version]
/product-owner make strategic decision on [feature]
/product-owner help with [product ownership challenge]
```

---

## Skills

- `backlog-management` - Grooming, prioritization
- `mvp-planning` - Core features, scope
- `release-planning` - Timeline, delivery
- `stakeholder-management` - Communication, alignment
- `product-vision` - Strategy, long-term goals
